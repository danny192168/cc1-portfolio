My Top Picks for Your Portfolio

  • Heading: Syne, Unbounded, or Space Grotesk — modern, technical, memorable.
  • Body text: Outfit, Manrope, or Nunito — readable, clean, with subtle warmth.
  • Accent / name logo: Righteous, Bebas Neue, or Playfair Display — instant personality.
  • Personal touches: Caveat for quotes, side notes, or a handwritten signature feel.
  • Code blocks: JetBrains Mono — clearly technical and very readable.
